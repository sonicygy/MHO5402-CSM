FROM REACTION TO CATEGORY

扮演顧客，試答一組問卷Be the customer: try a paired question

想像你入住酒店。這裡沒有標準喜好答案；分類器會根據你這一組反應作判讀。Imagine staying at a hotel. Your preferences have no correct answer; the classifier interprets this pair of responses.

這是你對一個屬性的一次回應，並不代表所有顧客。實際研究需要多位顧客的分類分布；Q 表示需澄清的回應，不是第六種顧客需要。This is one person's response to one feature, not a finding about all customers. A real study examines category distributions across respondents. Q flags a response to clarify, not a sixth customer need.
