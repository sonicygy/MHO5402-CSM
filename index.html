從顧客反應到需求分類FROM REACTION TO CATEGORY

扮演顧客，完成4組問卷Be the customer: complete all 4 question pairs

想像你入住酒店，為每項服務屬性選擇「提供」及「不提供」時的反應。4組、共8個選項全部必填；喜好沒有標準答案，不計入情境題分數。提交後，老師可彙整全班的分類分布。Imagine staying at a hotel. For each feature, choose your reaction when it is provided and when it is absent. All 4 pairs (8 selections) are required. Preferences have no correct answer and do not affect your scenario score. After submission, your teacher can aggregate the class category distributions.

每一組是你對一個屬性的回應，並不代表所有顧客。全班結果須按屬性比較分類分布；Q 表示需澄清的回應，不是第六種顧客需要。Each pair is your response to one feature, not a finding about all customers. Compare class category distributions separately for each feature. Q flags a response to clarify, not a sixth customer need.
